01 / FPGA · DATA ACQUISITION · POWER

Acquisition, at the edge.

A Spartan-7-based real-time acquisition platform planned around simultaneous-sampling ADCs, external memory, power domains and board-level interfaces.

PLATFORM DESIGN

Engineering focus

The platform targets multichannel acquisition and deterministic digital interfacing. The design work covers system requirements, FPGA bank and pin planning, converter interfaces, memory connectivity, clocking, board stack-up and power budgeting.

The implementation is being treated as a system-level hardware design: interface constraints, power and reset behavior, connector mapping and manufacturing documentation are considered together rather than as isolated schematic tasks.

More about the design decisions

Key considerations include separating noisy and sensitive power branches, planning clock and memory connections, budgeting IO banks and connectors, defining reset behavior, and accounting for controlled-impedance routing and return paths during placement.

Status: design and documentation work. This is not a claim of completed physical bring-up or product certification.
