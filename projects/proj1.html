Engineering focus

The platform combines an STM32F411 microcontroller with an INA226 current and bus-voltage monitor, USB connectivity and a four-layer PCB stack-up. The design emphasizes a repeatable acquisition path, clear grounding, power integrity and practical firmware communication.

The work includes schematic and PCB design, design-rule checks, manufacturing outputs, firmware integration and experimental acquisition. Predictive maintenance is the application context; the project is presented as an acquisition and validation platform, not as a completed anomaly classifier.

ENGINEERING NOTES / 02

Measured, documented, validated.

The work is organized around the acquisition path and the evidence available from the implemented platform.

A / ARCHITECTURE

Measurement chain

STM32F411CEU6, INA226 monitoring, 0.5 Ω shunt, USB CDC/DFU path and timestamped acquisition firmware.

B / PCB & FIRMWARE

Implementation

Four-layer 54 × 35 mm PCB, grounding and power layout, manufacturing files, bare-metal C with STM32 HAL and USB transfer.

C / VALIDATION

Experimental results

Acquisition demonstrated at approximately 664 Hz in the documented USB setup. This is an acquisition platform, not a completed anomaly classifier.
