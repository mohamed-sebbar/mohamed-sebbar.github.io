03 / ESP32-C3 · RF · POWER

A small system, end to end.

A compact four-layer low-power IoT plant-node design that brings wireless connectivity, battery charging, power conversion and motor control onto one board.

PCB DESIGN

Engineering focus

The design brings together a custom 2.4 GHz RF section, battery charging, a boosted 5 V motor rail, a dedicated 3.3 V logic/analog supply and sensor connectivity. The intent is to keep power conversion and RF layout constraints visible from the beginning.

The schematic, PCB layout and power-path choices have been developed as a design exercise. Assembly and hardware bring-up have not been completed, so measured RF performance and end-to-end behavior are not claimed.
